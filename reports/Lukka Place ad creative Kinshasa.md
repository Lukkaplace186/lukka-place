# Sell Kinois proof before selling dreams

For Lukka Place, the ad creative most likely to work in Kinshasa in 2025-2026 is built on proof. It is written in French, shows the price and entry costs openly, and ends in a WhatsApp conversation with the agent. Kinois renters' main grievance in this period's press is paying before they have seen a real house. Garanties run 6 to 12 months against a legal cap of 3. Commissionnaires take a $10 "frais de déplacement" and vanish. Videos show a house that turns out to be different or "déjà louée". People posing as owners sign fake contracts. Each poster should therefore answer one of those fears with a claim the product can actually back: listings checked by the team before they go online, agent numbers verified, a direct WhatsApp line to the agent, and entry costs written out. The buying path is Facebook first, then WhatsApp, so the paid base is Facebook with click-to-WhatsApp. TikTok is the growth channel and WhatsApp Status is free distribution through agents. Billboards are a poor buy while the city re-regulates outdoor advertising. The visuals should show real Kinshasa homes (a walled parcelle with its metal gate, a Gombe block) and Congolese people, with one royal-blue brand block and a headline of no more than seven words. The biggest creative risk is overclaiming. In Lukka Place's own system, "vérifié" means three different things. Putting an AI image next to a "vraies photos" claim would undermine the trust position. One caveat on the evidence: no DRC ad-creative test exists, and the Congolese press findings come from search excerpts rather than full articles. Treat the five briefs below as hypotheses to test on cost per WhatsApp conversation, not as proven winners.

## Kinois fear paying before they see a real house

Money is the deepest pain point, and it hits at the start. Loi n° 15/025 of 2015 caps the residential garantie locative at **3 months of rent** ([Radio Okapi, text of the law](https://www.radiookapi.net/sites/default/files/2016-01/loi_relative_aux_baux_a_loyers_non-professionnels_0.pdf)). The 2025-2026 press describes routine breaches. Actualite.cd ran a piece on the "phénomène 10 mois de garantie locative" ([Actualite.cd, Nov 2025](https://actualite.cd/2025/11/30/phenomene-10-mois-de-garantie-locative-kinshasa-un-opj-dhabitat-propose-la-creation-dune)). Radio Okapi reported landlords demanding **up to 9, sometimes 12 months**, with commissionnaires accused of inflating the count for their own benefit ([Radio Okapi, Jan 2026](https://www.radiookapi.net/2026/01/16/actualite/societe/location-immobiliere-kinshasa-certains-bailleurs-exigent-jusqua-9-mois)). An ACP survey found **8 to 12 months** common in several northern and central communes ([ACP](https://acp.cd/business/hausse-des-prix-de-maisons-en-location-observee-dans-des-communes-de-kinshasa-2/)). AllAfrica headlined the garantie as "un calvaire pour les locataires" ([AllAfrica, Jan 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601210556.html)). Rent rises sit on top of this, quoted in dollars. ACP recorded examples going from **$400 to $500 or even $600** in Gombe, Barumbu, Lemba and Ngaliema, while Kisenso, Kimbanseke and Mont-Ngafula stayed stable ([ACP](https://acp.cd/business/hausse-des-prix-de-maisons-en-location-observee-dans-des-communes-de-kinshasa-2/); [ACP, 2025](https://acp.cd/business/hausse-des-prix-de-maisons-en-location-observee-dans-des-communes-de-kinshasa/)).

The emotional core of the story is betrayal more than price. Tenants quoted by MediaCongo describe the two scenes an ad can stage almost word for word. The first: « On m'a demandé 10 dollars comme frais de déplacement pour aller voir une maison. Après avoir payé, le commissionnaire a disparu. » The second: « Ils te montrent une belle maison en vidéo. Mais quand tu arrives, soit la maison est différente, soit on te dit qu'elle est déjà louée. » ([MediaCongo](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-166209_crise_du_logement_a_kinshasa_en_2030_louer_une_maison_deviendra_un_luxe_alertent_des_habitants.html)). ACP's headline sums up the mood: the population has "une mauvaise image des courtiers immobiliers" ([ACP](https://acp.cd/societe/kinshasa-la-population-a-une-mauvaise-image-des-courtiers-immobiliers/)). Congo Quotidien notes that the trade is unregulated ([Congo Quotidien, Aug 2026](https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/08/04/hausse-loyers-kinshasa-commissionnaires/)). For buyers the fear is larger and slower to surface. A Kinshasa law firm logged **about ten cases in a single week** of people posing as parcelle owners who signed fake contracts and disappeared ([AllAfrica, Oct 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202610070624.html)). A resident described the same papers being used to sell one parcelle « à quatre personnes » ([Actualite.cd, Sep 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/09/06/spoliation-fonciere-kinshasa-pourquoi-les-poursuites-annoncees-par-le-ministre-de-la)). Online commerce coverage explicitly includes housing among the Facebook, TikTok and WhatsApp offers where anonymity hides fakes. That coverage concludes that confidence is what decides a purchase ([Congo Quotidien, Sep 2026](https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/09/19/commerce-ligne-kinshasa-arnaques/)).

The sources support four audiences with different hopes. Ordinary families and young workers in Limete, Lemba, Kalamu or Matete want an honest price and fewer months paid up front. One young worker reported his house going from $150 to $250-300 in two years ([MediaCongo](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-166209_crise_du_logement_a_kinshasa_en_2030_louer_une_maison_deviendra_un_luxe_alertent_des_habitants.html)). Low-budget renters start around **$50 studios with shared outdoor sanitation** ([Karib Afrik](https://www.karibafrik.com/blog/quels-sont-le-lments-connaitre-se-loger-kinshasa-)). Expatriate and NGO tenants in Gombe see asking rents of **$3,500 to $8,500** ([Alain Realty](https://alain-realty.net/bien/appartement-2-chambres-a-louer-a-kinshasa-gombe-residence-de-standing-avec-clubhouse/); [Immo24](https://immo24.cd/property/appartement-dexception-avec-clubhouse-a-louer-a-kinshasa-gombe/)). Diaspora buyers fear double sale and fake titles. The real proof of title for them is the certificat d'enregistrement, not a "fiche" or "livret" ([avocats.cd](https://avocats.cd/blog/acheter-une-parcelle-en-rdc-procedure-a-suivre)). The same poster cannot serve all four. The rental fear (paying for nothing) is the most widespread and the easiest to answer with what Lukka Place already does, so it should carry most of the five.

## Trust claims must match what the product actually checks

The trust signals that convert where scams are common are the ones that answer a named fear. "Agents vérifiés" answers the disappearing intermediary. "Écrivez directement à l'agent" answers anonymity. Real photos of the actual home answer the bait video. Openly written costs answer the garantie chase. Regional portals use the same toolkit. Property Centre in Nigeria and Kenya shows a "Verified" badge only after checking identity and business details, and still advises due diligence before paying ([Kenya Property Centre FAQ](https://kenyapropertycentre.com/frequently-asked-questions)). In Ghana, a study names lack of trust and the inability to verify as the main barriers to digital property platforms ([AJHSD](https://ajhsd.unilag.edu.ng/article/view/3188)). However, no independent study shows that a verified badge lifts conversion in Africa. Its value here is inferred from how strong the fear is.

The trap is that Lukka Place's own system separates three meanings of "checked", and a poster must not blur them. `approve_status` means a moderator judged the listing fit to publish. `agents.phone_verified_at` means the person holds that number, nothing more. `verified_at`, shown as "Vérifié par Lukka Place", means a human confirmed the property is real on those terms. That badge is set listing by listing, and no existing listing was backfilled (internal: `docs/claude/listing-data.md`, "Listing verification"). So "chaque annonce est contrôlée par notre équipe avant d'être en ligne" is true. "Agents au numéro vérifié" is true for the agents customers reach directly on WhatsApp. "Toutes nos maisons sont vérifiées" is false, and so is anything suggesting Lukka Place checks land titles. The same discipline applies to "sans frais de visite". Lukka Place does not control what an agent charges, and its own docs record that even asking agents about visit fees put off commissionnaires who were "already hesitant" (internal: `docs/claude/listing-data.md`). The defensible line is advice to the customer: « Ne payez rien avant d'avoir vu la maison ».

The same internal fact argues against the most tempting creative: mocking commissionnaires. Many Lukka Place agents are commissionnaires, so a poster that shames the trade as a group attacks part of the supply side. The poster should dramatise the bad experience (the vanished $10, the house that is "déjà louée") and leave the profession alone.

| Fear the poster answers | Claim Lukka Place can make | Wording to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Bait listing, "déjà louée" | « Annonces contrôlées par notre équipe avant publication » | « Toutes les maisons sont vérifiées » (only listings with `verified_at` carry that badge) |
| Intermediary who vanishes | « Écrivez directement à l'agent sur WhatsApp — numéro vérifié » | « Agents certifiés / identité contrôlée » (only the phone is checked) |
| Paying for nothing | « Ne payez rien avant d'avoir vu la maison » (advice) | « Zéro frais de visite » (cannot be enforced) |
| Garantie chase | « Garantie, avance, commission : écrites sur l'annonce » (where the agent states them) | Promising the 3-month legal cap, or summing the three costs |
| Fake seller (sales) | « Exigez le certificat d'enregistrement » (advice) | Anything suggesting Lukka Place verifies titles |

Imagery carries the same honesty test. Regulators converge on a simple rule. An image that could be read as the actual property is misleading if it does not match, and a disclaimer does not cure that. The UK ASA found an AI-generated persona misleading ([Osborne Clarke, Henry's Boots](https://marketinglaw.osborneclarke.com/advertising-regulation/henrys-boots-gets-the-boot/)). France's ARPP published labelling guidance, with wording such as « Image générée par IA », tied to the EU AI Act's transparency obligations from August 2026 ([ARPP guide](https://www.arpp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/030826-PRACTICAL-GUIDE-LABELLING-AI.pdf)). France's CEP flagged particular risk where the image itself is the commitment ([CB News](https://www.cbnews.fr/conseil/cep-appelle-vigilance-ethique-face-essor-ia-generative-publicite)). Audiences reject AI people most strongly. Nearly two in three US adults find AI-generated people unacceptable even when disclosed ([Gallup, 2026](https://news.gallup.com/poll/713222/americans-aren-sold-businesses-using-ai-advertising.aspx)). No African data exists, but a scam-wary market is the worst place to be caught. That gives the team four rules. Any home shown as "on Lukka Place" is a real listing photo used with the agent's consent. Agents and customers are real people, never generated. AI is allowed only for clearly illustrative mood scenes, and those carry a label. And no AI image ever appears on a poster that claims real photos.

## Facebook to WhatsApp is the path, so every poster ends there

Kinshasa already buys property-style through social commerce. Radio Okapi reports sellers of goods "même logements" moving to Facebook, TikTok and WhatsApp ([allAfrica / Radio Okapi, Sep 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609200024.html)). Agency Facebook posts in Kinshasa lead with "Prix : 1000$ - Garantie : 4 Mois + 1" and a WhatsApp number ([Facebook Marketplace listing](https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/item/1208551880735015/)). That is the format renters are trained to read, and a poster without a price, the costs or a WhatsApp route will look less useful than an agent's own post. The social audience is a minority. DataReportal counted **7.35 million social identities, 6.6% of the population**, in January 2025 ([DataReportal 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-democratic-republic-of-the-congo)). It is also lopsided: Facebook's DRC ad audience is **62.9% male**. TikTok's ad reach grew **+53.9%** to 19.3% of adults, while Instagram reaches only **2.5%** of internet users ([DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-democratic-republic-of-the-congo)). The buy follows from these numbers. Facebook (feed plus Messenger) with click-to-WhatsApp ads is the base. TikTok gets its own 9:16 cut. Instagram does not justify separate creative. A women-targeted ad set is worth running so the male skew does not hide female household decision-makers.

The best evidence for click-to-WhatsApp comes from one property portal. Lamudi's A/B test found that adding click-to-WhatsApp to lead ads produced **53% lower cost per lead** ([Instagram for Business, Lamudi](https://business.instagram.com/success/lamudi)). This is one comparable from outside Africa, read via a search extract, but it matches the local purchase path. Nigerian practitioners say WhatsApp ads fail mainly on slow replies and drop-off at the price question ([LinkedIn](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/growthmarketingagency)). That argues for showing the price on the poster and for routing the tap straight to the agent, which Lukka Place already does. Weight matters too. In Sub-Saharan Africa, data often costs **more than 7% of monthly income** against a 2% affordability benchmark ([GSMA 2025](https://www.gsma.com/somic/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/The-State-of-Mobile-Internet-Connectivity-2025-Affordability-of-Internet-Enabled-Handsets-and-Data.pdf)). That favours static JPG posters and short, sound-off video. Produce two masters: **1080×1350 (4:5)** for feed and **1080×1920 (9:16)** for Stories, Reels, TikTok and Status. In 9:16, keep the logo, price and CTA clear of roughly the top 14% and bottom 20-35% ([Billo](https://billo.app/blog/meta-ads-safe-zones); [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/recommended-aspect-ratios-by-placement-for-meta-ad-creative/)). WhatsApp Status is already the informal storefront for small sellers in East Africa ([TechTrendsKE](https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/05/16/whatsapp-african-women-business-livelihoods/)). A Status-sized poster that agents post themselves costs nothing.

Offline is a trust builder, not the acquisition engine, and the timing for outdoor is bad. Kinshasa froze new advertising structures in February 2025. In August 2026 the ARGPK presented a cadastre that removes at least half of non-compliant structures and steers main roads toward LED screens ([Decrypt-Eco](https://decrypt-eco.net/2026/08/25/rdc-kinshasa-impose-de-nouvelles-regles-au-marche-de-la-publicite-exterieure/); [Congo Quotidien, Oct 2026](https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/10/21/kinshasa-edit-affichage-publicitaire/)). If the team buys outdoor at all, short LED rotations on compliant sites are safer than long static contracts. Radio is concentrated: Top Congo FM reached **67%** of surveyed Kinshasa listeners in H1 2026 ([AfricaNews RDC](https://www.africanewsrdc.net/actu/radios-le-regne-sans-partage-de-top-congo-fm/)). That makes it a cheap single-station test, provided the spot says the WhatsApp number or a short URL aloud. These audience figures come from press reports of Les Points surveys, some of them written by the outlets being ranked. Every offline piece should carry its own number, URL or QR code so its taps can be counted.

## French headlines, Kinshasa gates and one blue block

French is the written language of commerce and media in Kinshasa, and Lingala is the street lingua franca ([Wikipedia, Kinshasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinshasa)). The local model is a French line with a Lingala echo. Orange RDC carried "Orange est là" into Lingala as "Orange aza" across its campaigns ([Beto.cd](https://beto.cd/rdc-orange-rdc-lance-sa-nouvelle-signature-orange-est-la-a-kinshasa-apres-lubumbashi/)). The Lingala property vocabulary is attested in real listings: **ndako ya kofutela** (house for rent) and **ndako ya koteka** (house for sale) ([IMCongo rental](http://www.imcongo.com/immo-congo-12214_maison_quartier_binza_pigeon_a_louer_kinshasa_ngaliema-immo-ln.html); [IMCongo sale](http://www.imcongo.com/immo-congo-19948_ndako_quartier_kinsuka_pecheur_ya_koteka_kinshasa_ngaliema-immo-ln.html)). A native Kinois speaker should still check any Lingala line before print. In French, the words with instant recognition are the ones renters live with: garantie, avance, commission, bailleur, frais de déplacement, parcelle, "déjà louée". Above all there is the agents' shorthand **"3 + 1 + 1"** (garantie + avance + commission), which reads at a glance as the cost of getting in (internal: `docs/claude/listing-data.md`; [MediaCongo listing](https://www.mediacongo.net/annonce-mediacongo-339410_immobilier_vente_location_toute_la_parcelle_a_louer_a_gombe_8000_dollars_contenant_une_maison_de_4_ch_piscine_grand_jardin_3_mois_de_garantie_3_mois_anticipatif_plus_1_mois_de_.html)). House rules carry into the copy. Write "référence", never "repère". Write a place as "quartier, commune". Keep "Parcelle" and "Appartement" distinct. Never add the three costs together into one total.

The tone should put proof first, aspiration second and humour a distant third. No Congolese test compares these, but proof is what the fear demands. Visually, generic stock lifestyle shots are reported to underperform: one agency put them at **0.6% CTR** without disclosing its method ([Blaccdot](https://blaccdot.com/blogs/best-real-estate-meta-ads-creative-lead-generation/)). African advertising commentary keeps returning to authenticity and local cues ([Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/en-za/node/425676)). For Kinshasa, that means the built forms locals recognise. Listings sell the « parcelle clôturée » with its « portail » ([Expat.com listing](https://www.expat.com/fr/immobilier/afrique/republique-democratique-du-congo/kinshasa/42-maisons-a-vendre/815106-parcelle-cloturee-avec-grand-maison-a-vendre-a-terre-jaune-nsele-pres-du-boulevard-lumumba.html)). Low houses with verandas fill most communes, and Gombe has taller blocks ([Universalis](https://www.universalis.fr/encyclopedie/kinshasa/2-organisation-spatiale-de-la-ville/)). Congolese adults should be shown in roles customers recognise: a couple at their own gate, an agent with a phone open on WhatsApp. Avoid Western suburbia and foreign skylines.

The typography follows Meta-style guidance for mobile: very little on-image text, high contrast, and one CTA ([Straight North](https://www.straightnorth.com/blog/meta-ads-creative-best-practices-that-convert/); [HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/create-powerful-ads-according-to-meta)). French runs **15-30% longer than English** ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/International/articles/article-text-size/index.en)), so write headlines in French at six or seven words, not translated. Set them in a bold or black sans-serif, very large, with no more than two short support lines. White on the brand blue #1650C5 measures **7.03:1**, which passes WCAG AAA ([W3C WCAG 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/#contrast-minimum)). So one system works for all five posters: a white field, a near-black headline and a single royal-blue band for the logo or CTA, inverted on one poster for variety. Repeating that block is how a brand-new name builds recognition across the series.

## Five posters, five different hypotheses

Five variants of one idea would teach the team nothing. Each poster below tests a different reason to choose Lukka Place, and the copy is a starting draft. Agency guidance on testing converges on changing one variable at a time and judging on cost per lead ([Walled Garden](https://walledgardenhq.com/blog/facebook-ads-split-testing)). Here the lead is a WhatsApp conversation, a tap that the platform already records per channel.

| # | Hypothesis tested | Audience | Draft headline (FR) | Support line | Visual | Format / channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anti-bait: "see it first" | Families, young workers in Limete, Lemba, Kalamu, Matete | « Voyez la maison avant de payer. » | « Annonces contrôlées par notre équipe. Écrivez directement à l'agent sur WhatsApp. » | Real listing photo: walled parcelle, painted metal gate, phone showing a WhatsApp chat | 4:5 Facebook CTWA + 9:16 Status |
| 2 | Verified human contact | All renters, anyone burned by an intermediary | « Un agent. Un numéro vérifié. Sur WhatsApp. » | « Pas d'intermédiaire entre vous et l'agent. » | Real, consenting agent photo with name and verified tick, phone in hand | 4:5 Facebook CTWA; women-targeted ad set |
| 3 | Cost transparency | Renters fighting the garantie | « 3 + 1 + 1 : vous savez avant la visite. » | « Garantie, avance, commission écrites sur l'annonce. » Optional example, labelled as an illustration: « Ex. : loyer 400 $ » | Typographic poster: huge "3 + 1 + 1" on the blue field, three labelled blocks | 4:5 feed; strong Status candidate |
| 4 | Price knowledge | Renters facing rising rents | « Combien coûte un 2 chambres à Lemba ? » | « Le guide des prix par commune, gratuit. » CTA to the price guide (`/combien-coute`) | Kinshasa street or commune scene (real photo); a real median only if live at print, otherwise no number | 4:5 + 9:16; rotate the commune per ad set |
| 5 | Local belonging, rent and sale | Broad Kinois; TikTok's younger audience; diaspora variant | « Ndako ya kofutela. Ndako ya koteka. » | « Gombe, Limete, Ngaliema, Lemba… trouvez dans votre commune. » | Real photos of a Gombe block and a low house with veranda; Congolese family at their gate (real people) | 9:16 TikTok and Reels; native Lingala check before print |

Three of these need a fact check before they go out. Poster 3 is honest only if a meaningful share of live listings actually carry `deposit_months`, `advance_months` and `commission_months`, which are NULL when the agent did not state them. Count that before print. If coverage is low, reframe the line as « Demandez le 3 + 1 + 1 avant de vous déplacer ». Any illustrative sum built on it, such as $400 rent at 3 + 1 + 1 meaning $2,000 before moving in, must be labelled as an example and never presented as an average. Poster 4 must show a figure only if it comes straight from Lukka Place's own price-guide medians on the day of printing. ACP's commune list ([ACP](https://acp.cd/business/hausse-des-prix-de-maisons-en-location-observee-dans-des-communes-de-kinshasa-2/)) is the right guide for which communes to feature. For poster 5's diaspora variant, the honest promise is a direct, verified line to a local agent plus the advice to demand the certificat d'enregistrement. Lukka Place does not check titles, so it must never claim to.

## Conclusion

The research reverses the usual property-ad instinct. In Kinshasa the dream home is not the scarce thing; trust is, and the creative that wins will do what the informal Facebook pages cannot do credibly. That means naming the cost of getting in, putting the customer in direct contact with an identifiable agent, and showing a house that exists. Lukka Place's advantage is real, but it is also narrow and precise. The poster copy has to be as precise as the database. "Contrôlée", "numéro vérifié" and "Vérifié par Lukka Place" are three different promises, and conflating them would turn the brand's differentiator into a new version of the bait it attacks.

The unknowns are large enough that the five posters work best as a measurement instrument rather than a campaign. No Kinshasa benchmark for cost per WhatsApp conversation exists, and there is no data on French versus Lingala lines or on how many women Facebook reaches in practice. Lukka Place already logs every tap by source. Two to three weeks of evenly funded Facebook click-to-WhatsApp spend across the five concepts would produce the first Kinshasa-specific creative data this market has. That data is worth more to the next brief than any comparable from Lagos or Manila.
