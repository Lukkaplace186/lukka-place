# Pain points, fears, desires and local vocabulary of Kinshasa renters and buyers (for Lukka Place ad copy)

> Method note: in this session, direct page fetching was blocked for every Congolese news host tried (mediacongo.net, actualite.cd, fr.allafrica.com, habarirdc.net: DNS not allowed). All findings below come from web-search result excerpts of those pages, not from reading full articles. Quotes marked "verbatim" were returned as quoted text in those excerpts. They should be checked against the original article before being printed on a poster. Dates: 2025–2026 unless marked **[older background]**. Items marked **[internal]** come from Lukka Place's own docs (`docs/claude/listing-data.md`), which record how real Kinshasa agents write their WhatsApp listings.

## 1. Most common frustrations when looking for a house or apartment in Kinshasa

### Takeaway
The pain points that recur most, in order, are: (1) the **garantie locative**, legally capped at 3 months but commonly 6 to 12 months in practice; (2) **commissionnaires** who charge travel or visit fees (about $10), then vanish or show houses that are different or "déjà louée"; (3) **rents rising quickly**, quoted in USD. All three are covered in Congolese media throughout 2025–2026. The core emotional story is "I pay before I've even seen a real house, and I can't trust who I'm talking to."

### Cited Findings
**Garantie / entry costs (the biggest money pain)**
- The law (loi n° 15/025 du 31 décembre 2015, baux à loyer non professionnels) caps the garantie locative at **3 months of rent for residential use**, 6 for commercial or socio-cultural use and 12 for industrial use. A landlord who takes more faces penal sanctions. — [Radio Okapi-hosted PDF of the law](https://www.radiookapi.net/sites/default/files/2016-01/loi_relative_aux_baux_a_loyers_non-professionnels_0.pdf); [Actualite.cd, 30 Nov 2025](https://actualite.cd/2025/11/30/phenomene-10-mois-de-garantie-locative-kinshasa-un-opj-dhabitat-propose-la-creation-dune)
  - Sources disagree on the penalty. Actualite.cd (2025) says 1 to 3 **years** of servitude pénale. [Le Maximum, Jan 2016](https://lemaximum.cd/2016/01/03/a-la-une/bailleurslocataires-plus-de-3-mois-de-garantie-locative-3-mois-de-spp-amende-de-3-a-6-mois-de-loyer/) says 3 **months** of SPP plus a fine of 3 to 6 months of rent. The article number also varies between sources (15 or 18). **Do not print a penalty figure without checking the official text.**
- Actualite.cd headline (30 Nov 2025): "Phénomène 10 mois de garantie locative à Kinshasa". In it, an OPJ from the Habitat ministry proposes a dedicated structure, and an official is quoted saying the garantie is "trois mois, pas plus, pas moins" (verbatim per excerpt). — [Actualite.cd](https://actualite.cd/2025/11/30/phenomene-10-mois-de-garantie-locative-kinshasa-un-opj-dhabitat-propose-la-creation-dune)
- Radio Okapi (16 Jan 2026) headline: "Location immobilière à Kinshasa : certains bailleurs exigent jusqu'à 9 mois de garantie locative". It reports that people describe "nine months, sometimes twelve months" paid up front, cites a father in Tshangu saying the 3-month rule is not respected, and says **commissionnaires are accused of inflating the number of months for their own benefit**. — [Radio Okapi](https://www.radiookapi.net/2026/01/16/actualite/societe/location-immobiliere-kinshasa-certains-bailleurs-exigent-jusqua-9-mois)
- AllAfrica-relayed article (21 Jan 2026), headline "La garantie locative – Un calvaire pour les locataires". It says "bon nombre de bailleurs exigent 6 à 10 mois de garantie locative" despite government reminders. — [AllAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601210556.html)
- ACP (Agence Congolaise de Presse), survey covering 23 Jul 2025 to 26 Feb 2026: "la garantie varie entre huit à 12 mois de loyer" in several communes, particularly in the north and centre of the capital. — [ACP](https://acp.cd/business/hausse-des-prix-de-maisons-en-location-observee-dans-des-communes-de-kinshasa-2/)
- MediaCongo: "des garanties locatives abusives, souvent fixées entre six et douze mois". A separate MediaCongo headline reads "Kinshasa : la flambée des loyers et les cautions illégales étouffent les ménages". — [MediaCongo 166209](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-166209_crise_du_logement_a_kinshasa_en_2030_louer_une_maison_deviendra_un_luxe_alertent_des_habitants.html); [MediaCongo 160711](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-160711_kinshasa_la_flambee_des_loyers_et_les_cautions_illegales_etouffent_les_menages.html)
- Another MediaCongo piece (undated in excerpt) says landlords set the garantie at "5, 6, 8 voire 10 mois". Its headline: "Garantie locative : la loi sur les baux à loyer impunément violée à Kinshasa". — [MediaCongo 140000](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-140000_garantie_locative_la_loi_sur_les_baux_a_loyer_impunement_violee_a_kinshasa.html)
- **[older background, 2018]** Actualite.cd headline: "RDC-Kinshasa : au lieu de 3 mois selon la loi, la garantie locative se négocie entre 4 et …" — [Actualite.cd 2018](https://actualite.cd/2018/08/21/rdc-kinshasa-au-lieu-de-3-mois-selon-la-loi-la-garantie-locative-se-negocie-entre-4-et)

**Commission (one month)**
- A legal commentary on loi 15/025 says agency fees at signing equal **one month of rent**, paid by the party who hired the agency. — [LinkedIn, S. Bahindwa](https://fr.linkedin.com/pulse/%C3%A9conomie-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rale-de-la-loi-n-15-025-du-31-d%C3%A9cembre-2015-bahindwa)
- BETO reports that a ministerial arrêté (Urbanisme) restated the one-month commission cap, **banned taking a commission from the bailleur as well**, and organised notice and eviction procedures. The article is about 1.5 years old, so check the current text. — [BETO](https://beto.cd/baux-a-loyer-en-rdc-le-ministre-de-lurbanisme-publie-une-nouvelle-reglementation/)
- A Karib Afrik housing guide says commissionnaires charge about **$10 frais de visite** plus a commission of one month's rent, **non-refundable**, and that a caution is also required. — [Karib Afrik](https://www.karibafrik.com/blog/quels-sont-le-lments-connaitre-se-loger-kinshasa-)
- Real listing wording on MediaCongo: "3 mois de garantie 3 mois anticipatif plus 1 mois de commission" (Gombe parcelle at $8,000). Another listing has "frais de commission à la charge du preneur". — [MediaCongo listing 339410](https://www.mediacongo.net/annonce-mediacongo-339410_immobilier_vente_location_toute_la_parcelle_a_louer_a_gombe_8000_dollars_contenant_une_maison_de_4_ch_piscine_grand_jardin_3_mois_de_garantie_3_mois_anticipatif_plus_1_mois_de_.html)

**Commissionnaires, visit fees, fake or already-taken houses**
- MediaCongo report (residents of Limete, Kalamu, Matete, Lingwala, Lemba) blames rising rents largely on "les pratiques jugées abusives de certains commissionnaires immobiliers". Tenant quotes, verbatim per excerpt:
  - « On m'a demandé 10 dollars comme frais de déplacement pour aller voir une maison. Après avoir payé, le commissionnaire a disparu. »
  - « Ils te montrent une belle maison en vidéo. Mais quand tu arrives, soit la maison est différente, soit on te dit qu'elle est déjà louée. »
  - Patrick: « Si l'État n'intervient pas, d'ici 2030, nous ne pourrons plus louer à Kinshasa. »
  - Source: [MediaCongo 166209 (Aug 2026 per search excerpt)](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-166209_crise_du_logement_a_kinshasa_en_2030_louer_une_maison_deviendra_un_luxe_alertent_des_habitants.html)
- Congo Quotidien (4 Aug 2026) headline: "Kinshasa : la flambée des loyers aggravée par les pratiques des commissionnaires". It notes that the trade is **not regulated**. — [Congo Quotidien](https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/08/04/hausse-loyers-kinshasa-commissionnaires/)
- Habari RDC (undated): headline "Trouver une maison à louer à Kinshasa, un casse-tête !". A companion piece, "Les commissionnaires : arnaqueurs ou intermédiaires utiles ?" (about Lubumbashi, ~5 years old), says commissionnaires are almost unavoidable. They charge about $10 "payable après une série de visites, que le candidat ait trouvé satisfaction ou non". The amount depends on the number of rooms "mais aussi de l'humeur du commissionnaire". — [Habari RDC casse-tête](https://habarirdc.net/trouver-maison-appartement-louer-kinshasa-casse-tete/); [Habari RDC commissionnaires](https://habarirdc.net/commissionnaire-arnaqueurs-courtier-logements-maisons-location/)
- **[older background, 2007]** Radio Okapi: commissionnaires « exigent des frais de déplacement à ceux qui sont dans le besoin, même lorsqu'ils sont conscients qu'il n'y a pas une maison mise en location ». Headline: "Le courtage ou commission dans l'immobilier divise les Kinois". — [Radio Okapi 2007](https://www.radiookapi.net/emissions-2/2007/08/30/le-courtage-ou-commission-dans-limmobilier-divise-les-kinois)

**Rising rents in USD**
- ACP survey (to 26 Feb 2026) found rises in Gombe, Barumbu, Lemba and Ngaliema. One example went "coûtait 400 USD, coûte actuellement 500 USD, voire même 600 USD". A Barumbu commissionnaire reported +$200–250 on large houses. Causes cited: strong demand, landlords raising rents without justification, speculation, scarcity, and demolitions of "maisons anarchiques". — [ACP](https://acp.cd/business/hausse-des-prix-de-maisons-en-location-observee-dans-des-communes-de-kinshasa-2/)
- An earlier ACP mini-survey (20 Mar to 23 Jul 2025) found rises in Limete, Lemba and Ngaliema and cited the depreciation of the franc congolais against the dollar. Prices were **stable** in Kisenso, Kimbanseke and Mont-Ngafula. — [ACP](https://acp.cd/business/hausse-des-prix-de-maisons-en-location-observee-dans-des-communes-de-kinshasa/)
- A Limete tenant (MediaCongo / Congo Quotidien): a house that cost **$150 two years ago now costs $250, even $300**. This is anecdote, not survey data. — [MediaCongo 166209](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-166209_crise_du_logement_a_kinshasa_en_2030_louer_une_maison_deviendra_un_luxe_alertent_des_habitants.html)
- Heshima Magazine (4 Mar 2026): "Kinshasa face à la vie chère : une capitale parmi les plus coûteuses d'Afrique". — [Heshima](https://heshimardc.net/v1/2026/03/04/kinshasa-face-a-la-vie-chere-une-capitale-parmi-les-plus-couteuses-dafrique/amp/)
- Housing deficit is estimated at about 4 million units (UN-Habitat, cited by Radio Okapi via AllAfrica, Sep 2026). — [AllAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202609040636.html)

**Quality and services (what people check)**
- Decent housing with water and electricity is rare. Limete, Gombe and Ngaliema have the most amenities but are largely occupied by expatriates. At the bottom end, shared outdoor sanitation is common (see §5). — [Karib Afrik](https://www.karibafrik.com/blog/quels-sont-le-lments-connaitre-se-loger-kinshasa-)
- **[internal]** Lukka Place's own listing fields reflect what agents and customers ask about: Courant / SNEL stable, groupe (generator), solaire, REGIDESO (water utility), citerne, forage, gardiennage, clôture, "accès en saison des pluies", and "frais de visite". When the platform asked agents whether they charge frais de visite, it "put off commissionnaires who are already hesitant", so the question was removed. — `docs/claude/listing-data.md` (Fiche pratique, utility tags)

### Inferences
- The strongest ad hooks are money paid up front for nothing (the $10 frais de déplacement and a commissionnaire who disappears) and the house in the video that is "déjà louée" or different on arrival. Both appear as first-person tenant quotes in 2026 press.
- "Garantie" is a highly charged word. Publicly promising a legal cap is risky because Lukka Place does not set landlords' terms. The safer angle is transparency: show the garantie / avance / commission split up front, before the visit.
- Rising rents plus the USD anchor make a "price guide by commune" credible: renters feel they cannot tell whether $500 in Lemba is fair.

### Gaps
- Full texts of the MediaCongo, Congo Quotidien, Actualite.cd and Radio Okapi 2026 articles could not be read because fetching was blocked. More verbatim quotes are likely in them.
- No source found for landlords demanding "6–12 months of rent up front" as **advance** (loyer anticipatif) as distinct from garantie. Press mostly reports garantie months. Listings show "3 mois anticipatif" separately.
- No systematic survey of how widespread frais de visite are, and no figure beyond the ~$10 anecdote and guide.

## 2. Words and expressions Kinois use (French and Lingala)

### Takeaway
House-hunting vocabulary is mostly Kinshasa French (maison à louer, parcelle, garantie, commissionnaire, bailleur, locataire, avance / anticipatif, frais de visite / de déplacement), mixed with Lingala on the platforms that use it (ndako, kofutela = to rent, ya koteka = for sale). Agents write entry costs as compact formulas like "3 + 1 + 1" or "4 + 1".

### Cited Findings
**French (Kinshasa usage, from press and listings)**
- "maison à louer", "parcelle à louer", "toute la parcelle", "appartement non meublé nouvellement construit", "garantie", "mois anticipatif", "mois de commission", "frais de commission à la charge du preneur", "concession" — real MediaCongo listing titles. — [MediaCongo listing 339410](https://www.mediacongo.net/annonce-mediacongo-339410_immobilier_vente_location_toute_la_parcelle_a_louer_a_gombe_8000_dollars_contenant_une_maison_de_4_ch_piscine_grand_jardin_3_mois_de_garantie_3_mois_anticipatif_plus_1_mois_de_.html); [MediaCongo listing 339874 ("loyer 900, garantie …", ref école La Borne, UPN)](https://www.mediacongo.net/annonce-mediacongo-339874_immobilier_vente_location_gs13_immo_sarl_met_a_votre_disposition_la_location_de_cet_appartement_non_meuble_nouvellement_construit_a_l_upn_ref_ecole_la_borne_loyer_900_garantie_.html)
- "ref" / "référence" as a landmark in listings ("à l'UPN ref école La Borne"). — same listing 339874. (Lukka Place house rule: say "référence", never "repère".)
- "frais de déplacement" (the word tenants use for the visit fee), "commissionnaire", "déjà louée", "garantie locative", "caution", "bailleur", "locataire", "calvaire", "casse-tête", "flambée des loyers". — [MediaCongo 166209](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-166209_crise_du_logement_a_kinshasa_en_2030_louer_une_maison_deviendra_un_luxe_alertent_des_habitants.html); [AllAfrica Jan 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601210556.html); [Habari RDC](https://habarirdc.net/trouver-maison-appartement-louer-kinshasa-casse-tete/)
- "courtage" (brokerage) and "Kinois" (as a self-label). — [Radio Okapi 2007](https://www.radiookapi.net/emissions-2/2007/08/30/le-courtage-ou-commission-dans-limmobilier-divise-les-kinois)
- **[internal]** How agents actually write on WhatsApp, recorded from real listings: "Garantie : 3 + 1 + 1" (garantie + avance + commission), "4+1", "500$ 4 mois+1", "+ 20$ eau et courant", "maison basse", "N portes", "N familles / ménages", "immeuble type locataire", "Maison Type Locataire", "Villa", "Terrain Nu", "700$ ata 650$" ("ata" = Lingala "even / at least", as in "negotiable down to"), "1800$ à discuter, 1600$ ça passe". — `docs/claude/listing-data.md`

**Lingala**
- **ndako** = house. — [mydialect Lingala dictionary](https://www.mydialect.io/en/guides/lingala-english-dictionary)
- **kofutela** = for rent (from kofuta, to pay). Lingala-language listings use "Kofutela – Ndako – Quartier Binza-Pigeon 1 500 $ / mois". **ya koteka** = for sale ("Ndako … ya koteka"). **biloko ya kofutela** = "things for rent" (the category heading). **Ndaku** is the variant spelling. — [IMCongo rental](http://www.imcongo.com/immo-congo-12214_maison_quartier_binza_pigeon_a_louer_kinshasa_ngaliema-immo-ln.html); [IMCongo sale](http://www.imcongo.com/immo-congo-19948_ndako_quartier_kinsuka_pecheur_ya_koteka_kinshasa_ngaliema-immo-ln.html); [IMCongo list](http://www.imcongo.com/congo-a-louer-tri--mode-list-recherche--congo-1-immo-ln.html)
- "Ndako ya mukie na biloko na ngo" appears on a furnished studio listing (≈ "house … with its furniture"). — [IMCongo](http://imcongo.com/immo-congo-12402_studio_mtaa_gb_yaku_panga_kinshasa_ngaliema-immo-ln.html)
- "commissionnaire" is used as a loanword in everyday spoken Lingala (an ASR speech dataset shows it next to "ndako"). — [Hugging Face Lingala ASR dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Svngoku/lingala-asr-dataset)

**Place names (communes and quartiers that appear in this housing coverage)**
- Gombe, Ngaliema (quartiers: Binza-Pigeon, Djelo Mbinza, GB, Kinsuka-Pêcheur, Macampagne), Limete (Mombele), Lemba, Kalamu, Matete, Lingwala, Barumbu, Bandalungwa, Kintambo, Mont-Ngafula, Kisenso, Kimbanseke, Nsele, Maluku, and Tshangu (the eastern district). — [ACP](https://acp.cd/business/hausse-des-prix-de-maisons-en-location-observee-dans-des-communes-de-kinshasa-2/); [IMCongo](http://www.imcongo.com/immo-congo-11588_ndako_quartier_mombele_kofutela_kinshasa_limete-immo-ln.html); [Zwandako Macampagne](https://zwandako.com/immobilier/studio-a-louer-macampagne-kinshasa-3/); [Radio Okapi Jan 2026](https://www.radiookapi.net/2026/01/16/actualite/societe/location-immobiliere-kinshasa-certains-bailleurs-exigent-jusqua-9-mois)

### Inferences
- Lingala hooks that fit the vocabulary above: "Ndako ya kofutela", "Ndako ya koteka". Short French plus a Lingala tag will likely read as local. Get a native Kinois copy check before printing any Lingala line. "Mokolo ndako" did not appear in sources (in the listings, "mokolo" means "day"), and "mosolo" could not be confirmed as housing vocabulary. Avoid both.
- Using "3 + 1 + 1" on a poster would be instantly recognisable to Kinois renters as the entry-cost formula.

### Gaps
- No usable corpus of Facebook-group, TikTok or YouTube comments surfaced through search, so street slang (Lingala-French "Indoubill" terms) for house hunting is not documented here.
- No source for a Lingala word for "commissionnaire" or "landlord" other than the French loanwords.

## 3. Target audiences and what each wants

### Takeaway
Sources support four segments: (a) ordinary Kinois tenants and families squeezed by rising rents and garanties in communes like Limete, Lemba, Kalamu and Matete; (b) young workers and students at the bottom of the market ($50–250 studios); (c) expatriates and NGO staff in Gombe and Ngaliema paying $2,000–8,500 a month; (d) the diaspora buying a parcelle, whose fear is double sale and fake titles.

### Cited Findings
- **Families and ordinary tenants**: the press frames rising rents and illegal cautions as "étouffent les ménages". The Tshangu father in Radio Okapi complains about garantie months. — [MediaCongo 160711](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-160711_kinshasa_la_flambee_des_loyers_et_les_cautions_illegales_etouffent_les_menages.html); [Radio Okapi Jan 2026](https://www.radiookapi.net/2026/01/16/actualite/societe/location-immobiliere-kinshasa-certains-bailleurs-exigent-jusqua-9-mois)
- **Middle class**: BETO headline "Kinshasa : le budget d'une classe moyenne qui ne respire plus". — [BETO](https://beto.cd/kinshasa-classe-moyenne-budget-cout-vie-invivable/)
- **Young workers**: the MediaCongo Limete quote ($150 → $250–300) comes from "un jeune travailleur". — [MediaCongo 166209](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-166209_crise_du_logement_a_kinshasa_en_2030_louer_une_maison_deviendra_un_luxe_alertent_des_habitants.html)
- **Low budget / students**: studios start at about $50 a month, but at that price sanitation is often outside and shared with about ten people in the parcelle. — [Karib Afrik](https://www.karibafrik.com/blog/quels-sont-le-lments-connaitre-se-loger-kinshasa-). Listed studios range from ~$60 in Macampagne (Ngaliema) to $250 in Lingwala, the latter with 5 months of garantie. — [Zwandako](https://zwandako.com/immobilier/studio-a-louer-macampagne-kinshasa-3/); [Nionsotoo](https://nionsotoo.com/listing/studio-a-louer-lingwala/). No student-specific source was found.
- **Expatriates / NGO staff (Gombe)**: agency listings show 2-bedroom apartments at $3,500 and $5,500 a month, a 1-bedroom with a river view at $4,000 ("idéal pour les expatriés"), a 4-bedroom at $8,500 with "3 mois garantie locative avec 1 mois de loyer anticipé" plus one month of courtage, and concessions at $2,000–6,000. Gombe is pitched as "l'un des quartiers les plus prisés et sécurisés". These are listing prices (marketing claims), not market statistics. — [Alain Realty](https://alain-realty.net/bien/appartement-2-chambres-a-louer-a-kinshasa-gombe-residence-de-standing-avec-clubhouse/); [Alain Realty river view](https://alain-realty.net/bien/appartement-de-luxe-1-chambre-a-louer-avec-vue-exceptionnelle-sur-le-fleuve-a-gombe-kinshasa-4-000-mois/); [Immo24](https://immo24.cd/property/appartement-dexception-avec-clubhouse-a-louer-a-kinshasa-gombe/)
- Expobeton (more than two years old) reported 3-bedroom apartments in the city centre at $2,000–4,500 a month. — [Expobeton](https://expobeton-newsletter.net/kinshasa-lalarme-sur-lenvolee-des-prix-de-limmobilier/)
- **Diaspora buyers**: a resident quoted by Actualite.cd (Sep 2026) on spoliation foncière: « La même personne peut prendre les mêmes documents, elle vend la parcelle à quatre personnes. » The seller then leaves for Europe. — [Actualite.cd 6 Sep 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/09/06/spoliation-fonciere-kinshasa-pourquoi-les-poursuites-annoncees-par-le-ministre-de-la). A lawyer quoted the day before: « Les poursuites ne suffiront pas, il faut surtout lutter contre l'impunité » (Me Grâce Tshiunza Tantamika). — [Actualite.cd 5 Sep 2026](https://actualite.cd/2026/09/05/spoliation-fonciere-kinshasa-les-poursuites-ne-suffiront-pas-il-faut-surtout-lutter)
- Buyer vocabulary and fears: certificat d'enregistrement (the only proof of title, registered with the Conservateur des Titres Immobiliers). Many parcelles sell on "simple fiche ou livret", which fuels double sales. A livret de logeur or attestation coutumière is not a title. Buyers should verify the title at the conservation, not trust the seller's papers, and measure the plot (surface overstated). Cheap peripheral plots (Kimbanseke, Nsele, Maluku) and long chains of intermediaries carry risk. Note that these are partly commercial sources (audit firms, agencies). — [Parcelle Safe Kin](https://www.parcellesafekin.com/); [Parcelle Safe Kin guide](https://www.parcellesafekin.com/guide-gratuit/en); [avocats.cd](https://avocats.cd/blog/acheter-une-parcelle-en-rdc-procedure-a-suivre); [AllAfrica Feb 2026 "Quelles sont les précautions à prendre avant d'acheter une parcelle ?"](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202602200584.html)

### Inferences
- Families and young workers want honest prices, fewer months up front, a real house at the visit, and no paying "pour rien". Expats want security, a generator or SNEL, water, and a vetted agent who answers on WhatsApp. Diaspora buyers want proof that the seller is real and the papers are checked, and someone trustworthy on the ground.
- Lukka Place's "agent vérifié" and direct WhatsApp contact fit the anti-commissionnaire message. For sales, a "verified agent" claim must not imply that Lukka Place verifies land titles unless it does.

### Gaps
- No quantitative audience data found: share of tenants vs owners, income levels, or diaspora remittance-to-property figures.
- No NGO or expat housing survey found. Gombe figures are agency asking prices only.

## 4. Trust, verification and scams in Congolese media

### Takeaway
Trust is the central issue. In 2026 the press reported fake owners signing fake contracts (around ten cases in one week reported by a law firm), commissionnaires who vanish after collecting fees, bait videos, and multiple sales of the same parcelle. Online-commerce coverage says Kinois rely on Facebook, TikTok and WhatsApp, where anonymity enables fake offers, so "confiance" is the deciding factor.

### Cited Findings
- AllAfrica (Oct 2026), "Multiplicité de cas d'escroqueries à Kinshasa – Un cabinet d'avocats lance un SOS": KTF Lawyers recorded about ten cases in one week. Swindlers posed as parcelle owners, signed fake contracts with victims, and disappeared with the money. — [AllAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202610070624.html)

### Inferences
- Copy that resonates: "agent vérifié", "vous parlez directement à l'agent", "la vraie maison, pas une vidéo", "pas d'argent avant la visite" (only if the product can actually enforce or show this), "prix affichés", "garantie, avance, commission : tout est écrit avant la visite".
- Avoid naming or shaming commissionnaires as a group. Many Lukka Place agents are themselves commissionnaires, and internal docs show they are a sensitive audience ("already hesitant").

### Gaps

- No Kinshasa-specific documented case of a WhatsApp "deposit to reserve" scam was found (only Benin).

## 5. Typical rent levels and entry costs (only what sources say)

### Takeaway
Reported figures range from a $50 studio with shared outdoor sanitation to $8,500 Gombe apartments. ACP press cites about $400 for a 1-bedroom (chambre-salon) and about $800 for a 4-bedroom, with examples of $400 → $500–600 rises. Entry costs are typically written garantie + anticipatif/avance + commission (e.g. "3 + 3 + 1" in a Gombe listing, "3 + 1 + 1" in agents' shorthand), against a legal garantie cap of 3 months.

### Cited Findings
- 1-bedroom + salon apartment around **$400**, 4-bedroom around **$800** (a press article citing ACP, per search excerpt; the original article was not identified). — via [ACP coverage](https://acp.cd/business/hausse-des-prix-de-maisons-en-location-observee-dans-des-communes-de-kinshasa-2/)
- Example rise: **$400 → $500, even $600**. Large houses in Barumbu +$200–250 (Jul 2025 to Feb 2026). — [ACP](https://acp.cd/business/hausse-des-prix-de-maisons-en-location-observee-dans-des-communes-de-kinshasa-2/)
- Limete house **$150 → $250–300** in two years (tenant testimony). — [MediaCongo 166209](https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-166209_crise_du_logement_a_kinshasa_en_2030_louer_une_maison_deviendra_un_luxe_alertent_des_habitants.html)
- Studio minimum **~$50** (shared outdoor sanitation). Visit fee **~$10**. Commission = **1 month**, non-refundable. — [Karib Afrik](https://www.karibafrik.com/blog/quels-sont-le-lments-connaitre-se-loger-kinshasa-)
- Studios **$60** in Macampagne and **$250** in Lingwala with **5 months** of garantie (listings). — [Zwandako](https://zwandako.com/immobilier/studio-a-louer-macampagne-kinshasa-3/); [Nionsotoo](https://nionsotoo.com/listing/studio-a-louer-lingwala/)
- Ngaliema / Binza-Pigeon house, 4 rooms, **$1,500 a month** (listing). — [IMCongo](http://www.imcongo.com/immo-congo-12214_maison_quartier_binza_pigeon_a_louer_kinshasa_ngaliema-immo-ln.html)
- Gombe: whole parcelle with a 4-bedroom house, pool and garden at **$8,000**, "3 mois de garantie, 3 mois anticipatif plus 1 mois de commission". — [MediaCongo listing 339410](https://www.mediacongo.net/annonce-mediacongo-339410_immobilier_vente_location_toute_la_parcelle_a_louer_a_gombe_8000_dollars_contenant_une_maison_de_4_ch_piscine_grand_jardin_3_mois_de_garantie_3_mois_anticipatif_plus_1_mois_de_.html). Gombe agency apartments **$3,500–8,500** (see §3).
- UPN (Ngaliema) new unfurnished apartment, loyer **$900** (garantie months truncated in the URL). — [MediaCongo listing 339874](https://www.mediacongo.net/annonce-mediacongo-339874_immobilier_vente_location_gs13_immo_sarl_met_a_votre_disposition_la_location_de_cet_appartement_non_meuble_nouvellement_construit_a_l_upn_ref_ecole_la_borne_loyer_900_garantie_.html)
- Garantie in practice: 6–10 months (AllAfrica, Jan 2026), up to 9 and sometimes 12 (Radio Okapi, Jan 2026), 8–12 in several communes (ACP, Feb 2026), "10 mois" phenomenon (Actualite.cd, Nov 2025). Legal cap: 3 months residential. Sources in §1.
- **[internal]** Agents' shorthand "3 + 1 + 1" = 3 months garantie + 1 month avance + 1 month commission, three separate costs (never summed as "5 mois de garantie"). "500$ 4 mois+1" = garantie 1, avance 3, commission 1. — `docs/claude/listing-data.md`

### Inferences
- A worked example for a poster (illustrative only, label it so): at $400 a month, "3 + 1 + 1" means $2,000 before moving in. At 10 months of garantie it is far more. The arithmetic follows from the sourced rent and month counts. Do not present it as an average.
- For the "guide des prix par commune", ACP names the communes where rents rose (Gombe, Barumbu, Lemba, Ngaliema, Limete) and the stable ones (Kisenso, Kimbanseke, Mont-Ngafula). This supports a "know the real price in your commune before you visit" message.

### Gaps
- No systematic rent survey by commune with medians found in public sources. Lukka Place's own price guide (market observations) may be the best data and should be cited from internal data, not from press.
- No current sale-price data (per m² or per parcelle) for Kinshasa communes found in this pass.
- The original source of the "$400 chambre-salon / $800 four-bedroom" figure was not confirmed beyond the search excerpt.
